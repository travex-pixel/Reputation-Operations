# Client onboarding checklist

Target: a new pilot client is fully set up within five working days of saying yes. Tick in order. Record dates in the client's folder and in the workspace client record.

## Before the call (day 0)

- [ ] Pilot agreement sent for e-signature (A$299, 30 days of Managed, nothing renews automatically, what we never do). See `research/08-compliance-and-risk.md` §10b for the outline; use the lawyer-reviewed version.
- [ ] Authorisation form sent (platforms, Reputrail account to be granted Manager access, what we may publish, approval rules, revocation). See `business/authorisation-process.md`.
- [ ] Invoice issued (A$299, due on signature).
- [ ] Workspace: prospect moved to **Won**; client record created from the prospect (`/workspace/clients`).
- [ ] Client folder created in Drive: `Agreement`, `Authorisation`, `Voice guide`, `Record`, `Reports`, `Requests (consent log)`.

## The 30-minute set-up call (day 1–2)

- [ ] Confirm the owner's first name for sign-offs and the business name as it appears on Google.
- [ ] How they talk to customers: greeting, sign-off, words they use ("the boys", "mate", "team"), words they avoid. Capture verbatim into the **voice guide**.
- [ ] What they will never say publicly (price, blame, specific staff names, warranty terms).
- [ ] Approval preferences: individual approval for everything, or standing approval for five-star thank-yous only (record it in the agreement).
- [ ] Escalation contact and hours: number, channel (SMS/call), earliest and latest time. Record on the client card.
- [ ] Three competitors they care about.
- [ ] Platforms in scope: Google, hipages, Facebook, ProductReview, anything else.
- [ ] Baseline: "Roughly how many hours a month do you spend on reviews and complaints now?" Record as `baselineOwnerHoursPerMonth`.
- [ ] Who sends completed-jobs lists for review requests, how often, and in what format. Confirm customers were told they may receive a follow-up message.
- [ ] Any live problem right now (a review that is bothering them, a dispute underway). Anything involving defects, injury, legal threats or money owed goes on the "owner handles with template" list.

## Access (day 1–3)

- [ ] Owner adds the Reputrail Google account as **Manager** on their Business Profile (Business Profile → People and access → Add). Never a password.
- [ ] Facebook Page: Editor role to the Reputrail account, if in scope.
- [ ] hipages: monitor only until the plan's reply mechanism is confirmed.
- [ ] Screenshot the access list; save to `Authorisation` with the date.
- [ ] Google Alerts set for the business name and trading names.
- [ ] Owner forwards Google review notification emails to the Reputrail inbox (optional but useful).

## First work (day 2–5)

- [ ] Log every existing review from the last 12 months in the workspace review log (platform, stars, date, first name, text).
- [ ] Classify them; mark anything sensitive for the owner.
- [ ] Catch-up plan: reply to all unanswered reviews over two weeks, oldest first, maximum five a day, each approved.
- [ ] First review-request batch prepared from the completed-jobs list, with consent records; send within 14 days of start.
- [ ] Competitor snapshot recorded (rating, count, reply rate ×3).
- [ ] Welcome email sent: what happens daily, how approvals work, the escalation promise ("same working day"), how to send completed jobs, how to stop.

## Day 20–30

- [ ] Day-20 conversion call booked: what we did (from the record), what changed, which plan fits, referral ask.
- [ ] Day-30 report produced from `/workspace/reports` and sent by the 3rd working day of the following month.
- [ ] Decision recorded: continue (which plan), change, or stop. Nothing renews automatically.

## If they stop

- [ ] Access removed within 24 hours; screenshot sent.
- [ ] Record exported and sent with the final report.
- [ ] Customer contact data deleted within 30 days; agreement and invoices retained seven years.
- [ ] Feedback and referral asked for.
