# Brand positioning

## Name and line

**Reputrail** (provisional pending ASIC, IP Australia and domain clearance; see `research/raw-name-clearance.md`). Tagline: **"Your reputation. In good hands."** Proposition: **"We look after your reputation, so you can look after your business."**

## Positioning statement

For owner-run trade businesses in Sydney who win work on reputation but have no one looking after it, Reputrail is a managed reputation service: a named person who watches every review, replies in your voice with your approval, escalates the bad ones the same day and helps you win the customer back. Unlike review software (which leaves the work to you) or marketing agencies (which treat reviews as a bolt-on), Reputrail takes responsibility for the outcome, month to month, with a written list of what it will never do.

## Category

"Managed reputation service" or, in plain speech, "someone who looks after your Google reviews". Never "AI reputation management", "SaaS", "platform" or "agency".

## Audience

Primary: owners of 2–30-staff air-conditioning, plumbing, electrical, painting, roofing and renovation businesses in Greater Sydney. They read on a phone, between jobs or after 7pm. They are proud, busy, sceptical of marketers, and personally stung by unfair reviews. Secondary: the office manager or partner who runs admin; referrers (accountants, suppliers, other tradies).

## Brand attributes and what they mean in practice

| Attribute | In copy | In design |
|---|---|---|
| Trustworthy | Say what we won't do before what we will; label every example as illustrative; cite or cut statistics | Hairline rules, record-like structure, no decoration that could be mistaken for proof |
| Human | "A person", first-person founder note, "we'll say so if we're not the right fit" | The founder's name and photo; a phone number |
| Commercial | Price on the page; "from A$299 a month"; the two-minute call | One action repeated, never three |
| Premium | Specific nouns, short sentences, no hype | Committed colour, confident type, generous space |
| Confident | Plain declaratives; no exclamation marks | Large display type, tight tracking |
| Calm | No urgency theatre; honest scarcity only (three pilots, true) | Deep green field, slow deliberate motion, one authored moment |
| Modern | Current platform names (Google Business Profile, not Google My Business) | Variable fonts, dark/light fields, no gradients |
| Precise | Timestamps, counts, dates in the record | Tabular numerals, mono for data only |
| Approachable | Australian English, "tradie" in body copy, "on the tools" | 6px corners, warm amber for the action |

## Voice

Plain Australian English, second person, specific. Short sentences. Nouns a tradie uses: quote, job, callback, call-out fee, ute, on the tools. Say "we" and "you". Avoid: unlock, revolutionise, next level, AI-powered, seamless, leverage, solutions, boost, guarantee, remove.

Three test lines: "Someone looking after your reviews who picks up the phone." "The two-minute call only you can make. We've done the prep." "We'll tell you honestly if a cheaper plan would do."

## Visual identity (summary; full tokens in `DESIGN.md`)

World: the dated, signed-off service record. Deep bottle green field (`#0F2C25`) carrying the hero, pricing and close; cool chalk ground (`#EEF1EC`) for reading; green-black ink; one stamp amber (`#E9A23B`) for the primary action and sign-off marks; a muted signal red for the negative-review marker only. Bricolage Grotesque for display and body; JetBrains Mono for record data. The mark is a service tag with a punched hole and a tick. Motion: the record stamps in once; everything else reveals quietly.

## What we never do (brand as well as compliance)

Buy, write or incentivise reviews; ask only happy customers; guarantee ratings, rankings or removals; publish without approval; ask for passwords; lock clients into 12-month contracts; fake testimonials, logos, results or urgency.

## Proof strategy while we have no customers

Show the mechanism (the record), the commitments, transparent pricing, a labelled illustrative audit, and the founder. Replace with real (permissioned) client stories only when they exist. Never imply traction we do not have.

## Longer-term direction

From "reputation" to "the customer-facing office you don't have": missed-call response, follow-ups, warranty and complaint coordination, review and reputation as the by-product. The name and the tag mark are designed to stretch to that.
