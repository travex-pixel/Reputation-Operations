# Reputrail founding customer pilot

**Offer:** the full Managed service for 30 days at A$299 (the Essential price), for the first three trade businesses in Greater Sydney to come on board.

## What the pilot includes

1. **Reputation audit** (delivered before you pay a cent): one page comparing your Google, hipages and Facebook presence with three competitors near you, what customers keep praising and complaining about, and the three things we'd fix first.
2. **Set-up call** (30 minutes): how you talk to customers, what you will and won't say publicly, who to ring when something's urgent, which reviews you want to approve individually and which (five-star thank-yous) we may post without asking.
3. **Authorisation**: you add Reputrail as a manager on your Google Business Profile from your own account and sign a one-page authorisation. We never ask for passwords. You can remove us in one click.
4. **Catch-up**: every unanswered review on your profile gets a reply in your voice over the first two weeks, oldest first, each approved by you.
5. **Daily monitoring** of Google, hipages and Facebook, every working day, by a person.
6. **Same-day escalation** of any negative review, with what we think happened and what we'd do.
7. **Customer recovery**: when a call could win the customer back, we prepare the call, suggest what to offer and log what was agreed.
8. **Genuine review requests** to every customer you nominate after a completed job (not just the happy ones), with a working unsubscribe.
9. **One-page report** at day 30: what came in, what we did, how you compare, what we recommend.

## What it costs and what happens next

- A$299 for the 30 days, invoiced at the start. Prices in Australian dollars.
- Nothing renews automatically. Around day 25 we talk. You can continue on Essential (A$299), Managed (A$499) or Premium (A$799), month to month, cancel any time with your plan ending at the end of the paid month. Or you stop, and keep the audit, the record and the replies.
- We ask two things in return: honest feedback on the first call and the last, and, if it earned it, a referral to one other trade owner.

## What we will never do (in the agreement)

Buy, write or incentivise reviews. Ask only your happy customers. Guarantee a star rating, a ranking or a removal. Publish anything without your approval. Ask for your passwords. Hold you to a 12-month contract.

## Who it is for

Owner-run air-conditioning, plumbing, electrical, painting, roofing or renovation businesses in Greater Sydney with roughly 2–30 staff, a Google Business Profile with at least ten reviews, and an owner who is willing to approve replies by text within a day and make the occasional two-minute call to an unhappy customer.

## How to start

Reply to this email with your business name, or request the free audit at the website. A person reads it the same day; the audit arrives within two working days.

## Internal notes (not for the client)

- Cap at three. Say so publicly and stick to it; the credibility of the whole positioning rests on the "never" list being true.
- Pilot delivery is set-up heavy (about 5 founder hours in month one). Schedule pilots at least a week apart.
- Day-20 call agenda: what we did (from the record), what changed, what the client noticed, which plan fits, referral ask.
- Conversion target: two of three to Managed.
