# Call brief: first three prospects (3 November 2026)

For a friend making first calls on Reputrail's behalf. Three businesses whose public reputation is visibly weaker than their work, with one true observation each. Research is snippet-level: before dialling, open the business on Google Maps and check the live rating, so the observation you use on the phone is one you have seen yourself.

## The rules (two minutes, not optional)

- Call the advertised business line, in business hours. Do not text, and do not ring a personal mobile.
- Say who you are and who you are calling for in the first sentence. Not selling anything today; offering a free one-page audit.
- Use one observation you have actually seen. Never invent a problem or a statistic.
- If they say no, thank them and stop. One call, one follow-up at most.
- Write down the date, who you spoke to, what they said, and whether they want the audit. That goes into the Reputrail workspace afterwards.

## Opener

> "Morning, it's [name], I'm helping a mate who runs Reputrail, a small Sydney service that looks after Google reviews and customer follow-up for tradies. Is the owner about? It's about the business's reviews, I'll be quick and I'm not selling anything today."

Then the observation, then: "He's doing free one-page audits for a handful of businesses: your numbers next to three competitors near you and the three things he'd fix first. Uses only what's public, no logins. Want one? Where should he send it?"

## 1. Pipe Perfection Plumbers, Marrickville (plumber, Inner West and Eastern Suburbs)

- Phone: on the website, pipeperfection.com.au (business line on the contact page).
- What we saw: Trustpilot 3.8 from ten reviews and Houzz 3.9 from seven, while aggregators quote a Google rating near 4.9. The low scores are what shows on the first page of a Google search for the name. Two pricing complaints sit unanswered.
- Say: "When you Google the business name, the first independent results are Trustpilot at 3.8 and Houzz at 3.9, not your Google reviews. Nobody has replied to the two pricing complaints there."
- Ask for: Darren (name appears on Yelp as owner; not confirmed, so ask for "the owner" if unsure).

## 2. Kmelectric, Leichhardt (electrician, Inner West)

- Phone: on the website, kmelectric.com.au.
- What we saw: the site claims 300-plus five-star reviews, but the first independent page people hit is Trustpilot at 4.1 from five reviews, and the Oneflare profile is empty. The company does reply on Trustpilot, so the owner cares.
- Say: "Your site says 300-plus five-star reviews, but the first thing that comes up for the name is Trustpilot at 4.1 from five. The audit would show how to make Google the first thing people see."
- Ask for: Ken (owner-named business; founded 2020).

## 3. Priority Plus Plumbing, Kirrawee (plumber, Sutherland Shire and St George)

- Phone: (02) 8999 5019 from a directory listing. One other source shows 8999 5010. Check the website, priorityplusplumbing.com.au, before dialling.
- What we saw: 12-plus staff, 25 years, a Leader feature and sponsored press in 2026, but no customer rating surfaced on any platform we checked. Top4 shows zero reviews. For a 24/7 emergency plumber that is the gap competitors with 1,200 reviews win on.
- Say: "Congratulations on the 25-year write-up in the Leader. When we searched, the press came up but no customer rating did. For a team your size doing emergency work, that is the first thing a stressed caller looks at."
- Ask for: Peter Honey or Con Papadimitriou (directors, named in the Leader feature).
