# Objection handling

Principle: agree with the true part, answer the real worry, offer the smallest next step. Never argue, never over-claim.

| Objection | What they mean | Response |
|---|---|---|
| "I can do this myself." | I feel I should be able to, and I don't want to admit I don't. | "You absolutely can; most owners mean to. The question is whether it's happening. When was the last reply you posted? If you've got the time and the stomach for the 9pm two-star review, you don't need us. If you'd rather it was just handled, that's the job." |
| "I don't get many reviews." | Not worth paying for. | "That's usually the problem rather than the reason. You've got [X] reviews from [Y] years of jobs; your competitors on [area] have [Z]. The asking is what we fix first, honestly and consistently, every customer you nominate. The audit will show you the gap." |
| "A bad review is just part of the game." | I've been burned and I've made peace with it. | "It is. What's not part of the game is leaving it there with no reply for the next hundred people who Google you. A calm reply and a phone call often turns it around, and when it doesn't, the reply still does its job for everyone else reading." |
| "I had an agency / Podium / hipages and it was a waste." | I signed a 12-month contract and got notifications. | "Yes, and I'd be wary too. Two differences: it's month to month, cancel any time, and a person does the work rather than sending you a dashboard. If it's not earning its keep after a month, you stop, and you keep the audit and the replies." |
| "Can you just get the bad ones removed?" | That's what I'd pay for. | "Only when they genuinely break Google's rules, like a review from someone who was never a customer, and even then Google decides. Anyone who promises removals is either misleading you or breaking the rules, and that risks your listing. What we can promise is that every bad review gets a proper reply and, where it makes sense, a call from you that often fixes it." |
| "Is this AI?" | Will a robot say something stupid under my name? | "AI helps with the boring bits: spotting new reviews and the first draft. A person edits every reply so it sounds like you, and nothing goes out until you've said yes. You'll see every word before it's public." |
| "I'm not giving you my Google login." | Trust. | "Good, don't. You add us as a manager from your own account, it takes two minutes, you keep ownership, and you can remove us in one click. We'll never ask for a password." |
| "A$299/A$499 a month is a lot." | Compared with what? | "Compared with hipages at A$139–649 a month for leads, it's the same money spent on keeping the customers you've already won. One extra [ducted install / hot-water replacement] a quarter pays for the year. And it's month to month." |
| "I need to think about it / talk to my partner." | Not convinced yet, or not the decision-maker. | "Of course. Would it help if I sent the audit and the one-page agreement so you can both look at it? What would need to be true for this to be an easy yes?" |
| "Send me some information." | Polite no. | "I'll send the audit, which is better than a brochure, because it's about your business. If it's useful, let's talk; if not, no follow-up from me beyond one check-in." |
| "Do you have other clients I can talk to?" | Prove it. | "Not yet. You'd be one of the first three, which is why the first month is A$299 for the full service. What I can show you is exactly how it works on your own listing before you pay anything, and the written list of what we'll never do." |
| "Winter's quiet, I don't need it now." | Seasonal cashflow. | "That's when the asking matters most, because the reviews you collect in the quiet months are what win the spring quotes. If cash is the issue, we can run monitoring only until September and switch on the rest when it picks up." |
| "I've got someone in the office who does this." | Already handled (maybe). | "Great, then half the job's done. Often what's missing is the escalation and the call-back when something goes wrong, and the monthly check against competitors. The audit will show whether there's anything left to fix; if there isn't, I'll say so." |
| "What if you write something that gets me in trouble?" | Liability. | "Nothing is published without your approval, we never admit fault for workmanship or safety in writing, and anything involving a legal threat, a defect or money owed comes straight to you with a template, not a public reply. We carry professional indemnity insurance." |

## Phrases to avoid

"Guarantee", "remove", "boost your rating", "rank number one", "AI-powered", "unlock", "revolutionise", "take your business to the next level", "limited time" (unless literally true).

## Phrases that work

"Handled", "a person", "your approval", "month to month", "the two-minute call only you can make", "we'll say so if we're not the right fit", "yours to keep".
