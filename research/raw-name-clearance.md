### 2.2 How the founder checks this directly (RECOMMENDATION)

1. **ASIC Connect – check business name availability.** Go to https://connectonline.asic.gov.au/ , open the business names register search and choose "Check business name availability" from the drop-down. ASIC's help pages: https://asic.gov.au/online-services/business-names/business-names-help/check-business-name-availability/ and https://www.asic.gov.au/online-services/search-asic-registers/business-names-register . The result is a traffic light: green = available, amber = ASIC needs to assess after you apply (common for made-up words such as "Reputrail"), red = unavailable. ASIC applies 14 automated tests (VERIFIED, https://www.connectonline.asic.gov.au/HLP/SearchRegisters/sch-using-this-service/sch-whatyoucansearch/check-business-name-availability/index.htm). Note: a business name is a trading name only and gives no proprietary right in the name.
2. **ASIC company and business name registers (general search):** https://www.asic.gov.au/online-services/search-asic-registers . Search "Reputrail", "Repu Trail", "Reputrak", "Reputrack" as a minimum.
3. **ABN Lookup:** https://abr.business.gov.au/ . Use the name search for "Reputrail" and variants; also check the "Business names" tab on any near matches.
4. Search both registers for the plural/possessive and spaced variants, because ASIC's identical-name rules treat some variations as identical.

Run quick searches for REPUTRAIL, REPU TRAIL, REPUTRAK, REPUTRACK, REPUTREK, REPUTRAIL*, and separately for *TRAIL in classes 35, 42 and 45, and for REPU* in all classes. Use the advanced search to filter by class and status.
3. **Classes likely relevant (ASSUMPTION, based on Nice class descriptions):**
   - **Class 35** – advertising, marketing, business management and administration for others. A managed reputation / review-management service for tradies sits squarely here. Primary class.
   - **Class 42** – SaaS / non-downloadable software, if any dashboard or monitoring platform is offered.
   - **Class 45** – legal and personal services; only relevant if the offer includes content-removal via legal processes or similar. Probably not needed at launch.
   - **Class 9** – downloadable software/app, only if an app is shipped.
   Sources: https://www.ipaustralia.gov.au/trade-marks/what-are-trade-marks/what-are-classes-of-goods-and-services ; https://www.signifyip.com.au/blog/trademark-classes-cheat-sheet-nice-classification .

IP Australia itself recommends consulting an IP professional (https://www.ipaustralia.gov.au/trade-marks/search-existing-trade-marks).

### 3.3 IP Australia fees (per class)

- **Standard application using the picklist: $250 per class.**
- **Standard application without picklist: $400 per class.**
- **TM Headstart: $200 per class (Part 1 pre-assessment) + $130 per class (Part 2 conversion) = $330 per class**; amendments at Part 2 cost extra (one guide cites $150 per class).
- Sources: IP Australia trade mark price calculator https://www.ipaustralia.gov.au/trade-marks/timeframes-and-fees/trade-mark-price-calculator (figures as repeated in search snippets; page not fetchable here); corroborated by https://legalvision.com.au/trade-mark-business-name-costs/ , https://www.markster.com.au/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-register-a-trademark , https://www.brandulegal.com.au/how-much-does-trade-mark-registration-cost-in-australia-2026-complete-breakdown/ . **VERIFIED (snippet-level); confirm on the calculator before filing.**
- TM Headstart Part 1 target: 5 business days; IP Australia reports 95% within 5 days and 97% within 7 days (https://www.ipaustralia.gov.au/about-us/our-customer-promise/timeliness). After the assessment there are a further five business days to pay Part 2. Headstart requests are not published until converted; the priority date is set on conversion (https://legalvision.com.au/trade-mark-headstart/ ; https://halfords.com.au/tm-headstart-guide/).
- **ESTIMATE:** two-class filing (35 + 42) via Headstart ≈ $660 government fees; via standard picklist ≈ $500, excluding attorney fees.

### 4.2 How to check properly (RECOMMENDATION)

- **auDA .au WHOIS** (summary of key fields): https://www.auda.org.au/au-domain-names/au-tools/au-whois/ ; full record: https://whois.auda.org.au . How-to: https://www.auda.org.au/au-domain-names/learn-about-au-domain-names/how-to-use-the-au-whois-tool/ .
- **auDA Domain Name Availability Check:** https://domaincheck.cctld.au (web) and port-43 WHOIS at domaincheck.auda.org.au; RDAP at rdap.cctld.au (VERIFIED, https://www.auda.org.au/news-insights/statements/auda-implements-rdap-protocol-to-access-public-au-registry-data/).
- Any auDA-accredited registrar's search box (VentraIP, Crazy Domains, Synergy Wholesale, etc.) for .com.au / .au, and a gTLD registrar or ICANN lookup (https://lookup.icann.org) for .com.
- .au domains cannot use WHOIS privacy/proxy services under auDA rules (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domain_privacy), so registrant ABN details will be public.

### 4.3 Eligibility rules (VERIFIED via auDA and registrar snippets)

- **.com.au / .net.au:** intended for commercial entities; the usual proof of eligibility is an ABN or ACN, and the registrant must be able to show how the domain relates to its business (https://www.auda.org.au/au-domain-names/au-rules-and-policies/au-domain-administration-rules-licensing-2/ ; https://ventraip.com.au/support-centre/eligibility-criteria-for-registering-au-domain-names/ ; https://www.crazydomains.com.au/help/article/cd-eligibility-criteria-and-requirements-for-au-domain-names). A trust cannot be the registrant; the trustee must be (https://support.opensrs.com/support/solutions/articles/201000063135--au-domain-policies).
- **.au direct:** open to anyone with a verified Australian presence (citizen/permanent resident, company, ABN holder, Australian trade mark owner, etc.). Launched 24 March 2022; priority period for existing .com.au holders closed 20 September 2022 (23:59 UTC); unclaimed names released to the public from 3–4 October 2022, so reputrail.au is first-come-first-served if unregistered (https://www.auda.org.au/au-domain-names/the-different-au-domain-names/au-direct-domain-names/ ; https://www.dentons.com/en/insights/alerts/2022/august/31/have-you-registered-your-au-direct-domain-name ; https://www.mcw.com.au/new-au-domain-deadline-what-it-means-and-how-to-register/).
- **Pending rule change (important for timing):** in August 2026 the auDA Board approved in principle removing the "close and substantial connection" eligibility pathway for .com.au and .net.au, replacing it with a test that the domain must match (or be an acronym of) the registrant's legal name, registered business name or registered Australian trade mark. As at mid-October 2026 this was a proposal awaiting public consultation on draft Licensing Rules, with no effective date; auDA reportedly stated on 19 October 2026 that existing licence holders would not be affected (https://domainnamewire.com/2026/09/08/auda-decision-could-impact-millions-of-com-au-domain-registrations/ ; https://millsip.com.au/auda-close-substantial-connection-test-removed/ ; https://www.sbs.com.au/news/article/auda-domain-name-change-new-rules-register-business/0z2da559u ; https://www.promptthemarket.com/auda-existing-com-au-names-new-rules/ ; https://www.ipwealth.com.au/blog/your-com-au-may-soon-need-a-trade-mark-what-audas-proposed-change-means-for-your-business). **Practical implication (RECOMMENDATION):** register the ASIC business name "Reputrail" (or a company of that name) *before or at the same time as* reputrail.com.au so the domain matches a registered name under either the current or proposed rules.
